# Competitions and leaderboards

Yearly challenges are the best way to see what actually works under a fixed protocol.

- **Waymo Open Dataset Challenges**: motion prediction, sim agents and the 2025 vision-based end-to-end driving challenge (WOD-E2E, Rater Feedback Score; won by Poutine): [Link](https://waymo.com/open/challenges/)
- **CARLA Autonomous Driving Leaderboard**: closed-loop evaluation on CARLA v2 scenarios; CarLLaVA and SimLingo report results on it: [Link](https://leaderboard.carla.org/)
- **CVPR Autonomous Grand Challenge (OpenDriveLab)**: multi-track challenge including end-to-end driving, NAVSIM and world-model tracks: [Link](https://opendrivelab.com/challenge2025/)
- **NAVSIM challenge**: pseudo-closed-loop planning on real data; Hydra-MDP and DiffusionDrive report results on it: [Link](https://github.com/autonomousvision/navsim)
- **Argoverse challenges**: motion forecasting and related tracks: [Link](https://www.argoverse.org/)
- **End-to-end V2X cooperative driving competition**: overview of challenges and results in [arXiv 2507.21610](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21610)
