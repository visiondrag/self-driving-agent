# Organizations and labs to follow

Groups that repeatedly appear in this collection. Each line gives the focus and a representative output; add a line per organization with a one-line focus when you extend this list.

## Industry
- **Waymo**: EMMA, Waymax, Waymo Open Dataset and the WOD-E2E long-tail benchmark: [Research](https://waymo.com/research/)
- **Wayve**: GAIA-1/2 world models, LINGO language-driving work, LingoQA, CarLLaVA: [Wayve Science](https://wayve.ai/science/)
- **NVIDIA**: Alpamayo-R1 reasoning VLA, Cosmos world foundation models, OmniDrive: [NVIDIA DRIVE](https://developer.nvidia.com/drive)
- **Li Auto**: Senna and DriveVLM-style dual-system VLMs: [Senna](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.22313)
- **Motional**: nuScenes and nuPlan: [nuScenes](https://www.nuscenes.org/)
- **comma.ai**: openpilot and the comma2k19 dataset: [openpilot](https://github.com/commaai/openpilot)

## Academic labs

- **Autonomous Vision Group (University of Tubingen)**: TransFuser, the tuPlan garage (PDM-Closed) and NAVSIM: [GitHub](https://github.com/autonomousvision)
- **HUST Vision Lab (hustvl)**: VAD, MapTR, DiffusionDrive: [GitHub](https://github.com/hustvl)
- **Thinklab, Shanghai Jiao Tong University**: Bench2Drive and Bench2Drive-VL closed-loop benchmarks: [Bench2Drive](https://github.com/Thinklab-SJTU/Bench2Drive)
- **MetaDriverse (CUHK and collaborators)**: MetaDrive, ScenarioNet: [MetaDrive](https://github.com/metadriverse/metadrive)
- **OpenDILab**: LMDrive closed-loop language driving: [LMDrive](https://github.com/opendilab/LMDrive)
