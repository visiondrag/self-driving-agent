# Learning

Suggested path: read a survey, take one robotics course and one deep learning course, then follow the [reading paths](../README.md#suggested-reading-paths) from the README.

## Surveys first
- **End-to-end Autonomous Driving: Challenges and Frontiers**: the standard entry point: [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.16927)
- **A Survey on Vision-Language-Action Models for Autonomous Driving**: the VLA overview: [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.24044)
- **Vision-Language-Action Models for Autonomous Driving: Past, Present, and Future**: a late-2025 update: [Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.16760)
- More in [Surveys & roadmaps](surveys.md).

## Courses
- **Self-Driving Cars Specialization (University of Toronto, Coursera)**: state estimation, visual perception, motion planning: [Link](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/self-driving-cars)
- **MIT 6.S094: Deep Learning for Self-Driving Cars**: lecture series and course site: [Link](https://selfdrivingcars.mit.edu/)

## Books
- **Probabilistic Robotics**, S. Thrun, W. Burgard, D. Fox: filtering, localization and mapping foundations: [Link](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262201629/probabilistic-robotics/)
- **Planning Algorithms**, S. LaValle (free online): motion planning and decision making: [Link](https://lavalle.pl/planning/)
- **Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction**, R. Sutton and A. Barto (free online): background for the RL post-training papers: [Link](http://incompleteideas.net/book/the-book-2nd.html)
